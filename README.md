## 🎓 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student** at the [Institute of Artificial Intelligence (IAI)](https://ai.ucf.edu/), **University of Central Florida**, advised by [Dr. Chen Chen](https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/chenchen/). I build **multimodal AI systems** that perceive, generate, and act. My research evolved from visual-temporal perception (CVPR Oral'22, CVPR'23) through generative and world modeling (3DV'24, NeurIPS'25) to my current focus on efficient multimodal and long-sequence inference and LLM-based agentic systems (ICLR'26, NeurIPS'26 ×2, ICASSP'26, ECCV'26). I completed my Master's at ShanghaiTech University under Prof. [Shenghua Gao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=fe-1v0MAAAAJ).

🔬 **Research Interests:**
- **Efficient Multimodal Inference**: Token Pruning, Long-Video VLMs, Joint Frame/Pixel/Latency Allocation
- **LLM Agents**: Multi-Agent Orchestration, Tool Use, Planning & Memory, Agent Evaluation
- **Generative & World Modeling**: Long-form Video Generation, Motion Generation, 3D Scene Generation
- **Video Understanding**: Temporal Modeling, Repetitive Action Counting, Weakly Supervised Learning
- **Time Series & Sequential Modeling**: Multimodal Forecasting, Long-History Recommendation

🎯 **Current Focus**: *System-algorithm co-design for agent harnesses* — the inference, memory, and orchestration layer that lets long-horizon multimodal agents reason and act reliably.
